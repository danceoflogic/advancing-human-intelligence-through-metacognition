# Ethics and privacy

This public repository concerns cognitive strategies, **not** diagnosis, medical treatment, or a validated IQ intervention.

## Safeguards

- Never publish identifiable participant records, health or medication history, private conversations, or sensitive demographic data.
- Before recruiting anyone, use informed consent and perform an appropriate ethics and data-protection assessment. Additional safeguards are required for children or vulnerable groups.
- Use pseudonyms where helpful but do not assume pseudonymisation equals anonymity.
- Protect held-out test materials from exposure.
- Maintain an audit trail of amendments, exclusions, failures, and adverse events.
- A public GitHub repository does not replace independent or institutional ethics review where required.

## AI-assisted work

AI tools may assist with organisation, drafting, and analysis, but citations, measurements, numerical calculations, methods, and conclusions require independent verification. Clearly label simulated or unverified content.
