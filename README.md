# Advancing Human Intelligence through Better Metacognition

**Status:** Exploratory public research project · **Started:** 2026-10-11

> **Central question:** Can people improve the quality of their reasoning, learning, and decisions by deliberately changing how they monitor, select, and refine their thinking strategies?

This project studies **effective cognitive performance**: how successfully people use the cognitive resources available to them in real tasks. It investigates whether deliberately trained metacognitive strategies can improve problem solving, reasoning accuracy, error detection, confidence calibration, and learning.

The project **does not assume** that intelligence is infinitely malleable, that raw cognitive ability is irrelevant, or that practising thinking strategies raises IQ. Near and far transfer are open empirical questions.

## Where the idea came from

Children learn more than facts. They may also acquire ways of asking questions, judging evidence, planning, and revising beliefs through experience and social learning. If some thinking habits are learned, perhaps adults can also refine them deliberately.

Our initial engineering analogy is a **cognitive strategy router**: classify a problem, select an appropriate thinking strategy, monitor progress, verify outcomes, and improve future choices. This is a **proposed design model**, not an established account of brain function.

## Research directions

1. Map the evidence on metacognition, self-regulated learning, reasoning strategies, and limits of transfer.
2. Develop an explicit and falsifiable model of task-dependent strategy selection.
3. Design training exercises and objective measures of performance.
4. Compare performance on practised, similar untrained, and distinctly different tasks.
5. Document null findings, confounders, and limits as carefully as positive results.

## Principles

**Separate evidence, interpretation, and speculation.** Use held-out tasks and clearly defined outcomes. Do not equate stronger use of a technique with increased intelligence. Prioritise reproducibility, privacy, legitimate sources, and honest reporting.

**Current stage:** Conceptual scaffold, version 0.1. No original experiment has been completed and no empirical improvement is claimed.

Open issues and contributions are welcome. This is a public research repository, not an archive for anyone's private cognitive or health data.
