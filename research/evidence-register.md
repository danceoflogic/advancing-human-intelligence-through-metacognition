# Initial evidence register

This is a **starter selection**, not a systematic review. Read primary sources before drawing fine-grained causal conclusions.

## E001 · Metacognition in education

**Source:** Education Endowment Foundation. *Metacognition and Self-Regulated Learning* (guidance report).
https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/metacognition

**Relevance:** Planning, monitoring, and evaluation can support academic learning. **Boundary:** Educational attainment does not imply higher general intelligence or adult far transfer.

## E002 · Learning strategies vary in usefulness

**Source:** Dunlosky, J., et al. (2013). *Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques*. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58.
https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266

**Relevance:** Evidence differs by learning technique. **Boundary:** Effective study methods are not equivalent to general intelligence gains.

## E003 · Strategies depend on learning phase

**Source:** Hattie, J. A. C. & Donoghue, G. M. (2016). *Learning strategies: a synthesis and conceptual model*. npj Science of Learning, 1, 16013.
https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.13

**Relevance:** Strategy usefulness depends on context and learning stage. **Boundary:** This does not validate the particular strategy router model proposed here.

## E004 · Limits of cognitive training transfer

**Source:** Melby-Lervåg, M., Redick, T. S. & Hulme, C. (2016). *Working Memory Training Does Not Improve Performance on Measures of Intelligence or Other Measures of “Far Transfer”*. Perspectives on Psychological Science, 11(4), 512–534.
https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691616635612

**Relevance:** A caution about generalising improvements beyond trained tasks. **Boundary:** Working-memory training evidence does not decide whether all metacognitive interventions can transfer.

## Evidence standards

For each additional study, record author, year, title, DOI/URL, design, population, measures, effect sizes where available, limitations and counterevidence. Do not invent unverified conclusions.

## Open gaps

Adult reasoning interventions; explicit strategy selection; cross-domain transfer; long-term retention; parent modelling versus confounders.
