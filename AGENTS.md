# Instructions for AI agents

Before significant changes, read `INDEX.md`, `README.md`, `docs/project-charter.md`, and `research/evidence-register.md`.

1. Treat this as a public, evidence-oriented research repository, not the private Brain archive.
2. Do not assert that IQ can be increased or far transfer has been demonstrated without compelling evidence.
3. Distinguish observations, interpretation, and speculation.
4. Never invent citations, data, or study outcomes.
5. Preserve counterevidence and null findings.
6. Use Markdown; prefer CSV/JSON for structured data; kebab-case filenames; ISO dates.
7. Never copy personal records, secrets, or unpublished participant data here.
8. Keep changes auditable and revise `INDEX.md` and `ROADMAP.md` when structure changes.
9. Do not autonomously recruit participants or run human studies.
10. Treat the strategy-routing analogy as a hypothesis to be tested, not a fact about the brain.
