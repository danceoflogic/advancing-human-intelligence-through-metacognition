# Research questions and hypotheses

**These are proposals, not findings.** Specify measurements, exclusions, stopping rules and analysis decisions before any confirmatory data collection.

| ID | Hypothesis | Candidate independent outcome | Major alternative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H01 | A plan-monitor-evaluate routine improves reasoning on similar unseen tasks. | Scored accuracy | Practice or extra time |
| H02 | Task-specific strategy selection outperforms using the same checklist everywhere. | Accuracy and time on counterbalanced tasks | Additional engagement |
| H03 | Confidence feedback improves calibration. | Brier score on unseen questions | Knowledge gains |
| H04 | Some gains persist after training. | Follow-up on held-out items | Continued informal practice |
| H05 | Some improvements transfer across distinct domains. | Predefined far-transfer tests | Hidden task similarity |
| H06 | Childhood social modelling affects later reasoning habits. | Separate literature review and study | Genetic and social confounding |

## Outcome hierarchy

Primary: preregistered objectively scored measure on unseen items. Secondary: response time, error classes, calibration. Exploratory: adherence and self-reported usefulness.

## Avoid circularity

Using the strategy more often is not in itself evidence of becoming more intelligent. Keep process measures and independently scored outcomes separate.

Date, version, and label every protocol change as pre-data or post-data.
