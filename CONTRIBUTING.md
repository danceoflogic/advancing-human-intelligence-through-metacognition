# Contributing

Evidence-based corrections and critical perspectives are welcome, especially negative findings and sources that challenge the project's hypotheses.

Contributions might include citation corrections, replication failures, risk-of-bias concerns, alternative explanations, or better independent performance measures.

To contribute, open an Issue stating the claim or problem, supporting evidence, proposed change, and remaining uncertainty.

Keep comments respectful and scientific. **Do not post private test results, identifying information, or health records.** Use Markdown, lowercase kebab-case filenames, ISO dates, and explicitly separate evidence from interpretation and speculation.

Only submit material you have permission to share. Original project documentation is covered by [the CC BY 4.0 documentation licence](LICENSE.md); external source rights remain with their owners.
